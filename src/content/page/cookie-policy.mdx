      ## What Cookies Are

      Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow the site to recognize your device and remember information about your visit, such as your preferences or login state. Similar technologies include web beacons, pixels, and local storage.

      ## Categories of Cookies We Use

      The Site uses the following categories of cookies:

      ### Strictly Necessary Cookies

      These cookies are essential to the operation of the Site. They enable core functions such as session management and security features. The Site cannot function properly without them.

      ### Performance and Analytics Cookies

      These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with the Site by collecting information such as pages visited, time spent on the Site, and any errors encountered. This information is used in aggregate form to improve the Site.

      ### Functionality Cookies

      These cookies allow the Site to remember choices you make, such as your preferences and settings, in order to provide a more personalized experience on subsequent visits.

      ### Advertising and Third-Party Cookies

      These cookies may be set by third parties whose services we use on the Site. They may collect information about your browsing habits to deliver relevant advertising and to measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns.

      ## Third-Party Cookies

      Some cookies on the Site are set by third parties whose services we use, such as analytics providers and advertising networks. These third parties may collect information about your online activities over time and across different websites. We do not control these third-party cookies, and their use is governed by the privacy policies of the parties that set them.

      ## Managing Cookies

      Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can typically set your browser to accept or reject cookies, to notify you when a cookie is set, or to delete cookies that have already been set. Please note that if you choose to reject or delete certain cookies, some features of the Site may not function properly.

      You may also be able to opt out of certain third-party analytics and advertising cookies through industry opt-out mechanisms such as those provided by the Digital Advertising Alliance and the Network Advertising Initiative.
