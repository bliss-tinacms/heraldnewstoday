Political and international stories can move quickly, but speed without context leaves readers with fragments. Our approach connects the latest development to the institutions, timelines, and prior decisions that shape it.

## Identify what changed

We begin by separating a new event from the conditions that already existed. That makes it easier to understand whether an announcement changes policy, begins a process, or simply restates a position.

## Name the source of authority

Governments and institutions distribute authority across many offices. We identify who can act, what approvals are required, and which consequences are immediate or conditional.

## Update transparently

Fast-moving stories may change after publication. Material additions or corrections are reflected clearly so readers can distinguish the original facts from later developments.
