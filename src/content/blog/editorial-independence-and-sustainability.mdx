A publication needs both a durable business and an independent newsroom. Those responsibilities support one another, but they are not the same job.

At Herald News Today, the publisher oversees overall direction and financial sustainability from the commercial side. Daily decisions about what to cover, how to report it, and when a story is ready to publish remain with the newsroom.

## Why the separation matters

Readers should be able to trust that a story was selected for its public value rather than its commercial convenience. A clear boundary also helps reporters evaluate claims and sources without having to anticipate business preferences.

## What the publisher is responsible for

The publishing role includes long-term planning, operational support, and the financial health needed to keep reporting available. It also includes defending the conditions in which editors can make sound judgments.

## What readers can expect

Herald News Today will continue to explain its standards, distinguish reporting from opinion or promotion, and correct material errors clearly. Questions or concerns about our work can be sent through the Contact page.
