Accuracy begins before publication. It requires checking names, dates, quotations, documents, and the context in which a claim appears.

## Verification before speed

Timeliness matters, but a fast answer is not useful if it is wrong. We look for source material that can be examined directly, identify where information came from, and avoid presenting an unresolved claim as an established fact.

## Clear attribution

Readers should be able to understand who is making a claim and what evidence supports it. When a source cannot be identified publicly, that limitation should be explained as clearly as circumstances allow.

## Updates and corrections

Developing stories can change. We update coverage when new verified information materially improves the report. When we make a material error, we correct the story and make the change understandable rather than quietly leaving readers to guess what happened.

Readers can report a possible error through our Contact page. Please include the article URL, the passage at issue, and any supporting material.
