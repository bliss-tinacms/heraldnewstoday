Technology coverage should help readers understand consequences, not simply repeat launch announcements. We look at the systems, incentives, and rules surrounding a product or platform.

## Products in context

A new service matters because of what it changes: a workflow, a market, a relationship, a public institution, or a person's daily routine. We connect technical developments to those practical effects.

## Evidence before prediction

Technology stories often arrive with ambitious claims. We distinguish demonstrated capability from forecasts and identify the evidence available at publication time.

## Policy and accountability

Privacy, competition, accessibility, labor, and public oversight can be as important as specifications. Our reporting follows those questions as technology moves from novelty into infrastructure.
